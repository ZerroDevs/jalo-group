Our Journey & Purpose

Founded in the heart of Libya's energy heartland, Jalo Group for Oil & Gas Services was established to bridge the gap between demanding international energy standards and localized execution capabilities.

Over years of continuous field operations, we have evolved from specialized maintenance contractors into a trusted full-spectrum partner for both National Oil Corporation (NOC) operating entities and multinational energy operators.
